Privacy Policy

Ricotta Orders · Last updated 31 October 2026

Who uses this app

Ricotta Orders is the private ordering app of the Ricotta kitchen in Erbil. Only the kitchen’s two staff accounts can sign in; there is no public sign-up. The kitchen manager, Rozha, is responsible for the data in it.

There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data is sold or shared for marketing. The app does keep two records about its own use, explained below: time spent in the app, and error reports.

What the app keeps, and why

  • Your account: your name and your 6-digit PIN. The PIN is stored only as a one-way scramble (bcrypt), so nobody can read it back.
  • Signing in: a sign-in token (scrambled), a random ID for this device, its type (for example iPhone), the app version and when it was last used. Each PIN try is kept with a scrambled form of the network address, to stop anyone guessing PINs.
  • The kitchen’s work: suppliers (names, phone numbers, reminder times), items, units, orders (what was ordered, by whom and when), unsent drafts, a record of who changed items and suppliers, the kitchen streak and a summary of each finished month.
  • Rico: chats you keep with Rico, the messages Rico writes to you, and a count of each AI request (which model and how long), used for the hourly limit.
  • Time in the app: how many seconds the app was on screen and in use, per person, phone and day. Rozha sees these totals in Admin.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the error’s own text, the screen, the phone type and the app version, under the person who was signed in (or “before sign-in”). PINs, sign-in tokens, keys and long numbers are removed before anything is kept.
  • Rico’s test orders: the orders Rico sends to Rozha’s own WhatsApp while testing (supplier, items, amounts and the reason).
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the delivery address your phone gives and your language.

Voice messages to Rico and photos of item lists are only sent to be read and are not kept by Ricotta.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores the data and runs the app’s server. GitHub Pages serves the app’s files.
  • Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini and Groq (AI services) write Rico’s replies; Google Gemini and Groq also read voice messages and item lists. They receive what you write or say to Rico and the order details he needs (items, suppliers and quantities), never your PIN. This happens only after you allow it, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the orders Rico sends by himself when Rozha asks him. While testing, these messages go only to Rozha’s own WhatsApp number. They contain the supplier, the items and the quantities, nothing else.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile may run a quick security check when you sign in.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla deliver notifications to your device, if you turn them on.
  • WhatsApp opens with the order message when you choose to send an order; WhatsApp’s own policy applies there.
  • Open-Meteo gives the weather for Erbil; no personal data is sent to it.

Each of these services is used only to run the app and is expected to protect the data at least as well as this policy.

How long it is kept

  • Orders: 60 days, then they are deleted. Each finished month also becomes one summary. The record of changes: the current month.
  • Rico’s messages and reminder marks: 30 days. AI request counts, Rico’s test orders, and saved Rico chats (after their last use): 90 days.
  • Error reports: up to 120 days (30 days once fixed). Time in the app: kept as a running total, with no end date.
  • Sign-ins end after 18 hours and are deleted 2 days later. PIN tries: 2 days. Devices that signed out and stayed unused: 30 days.
  • Accounts, suppliers, items, units, settings, drafts, the streak and month summaries: until they are deleted.

Your choices

  • Turn Rico’s AI off or on in Settings › Privacy. Turn notifications off in Settings.
  • Sign out from the account button at the top of the app.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, or to take back any permission, ask Rozha, the kitchen manager, at rozhzero969@gmail.com. She can delete it in the app or remove it from the database.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. The database is locked so that only the app’s own server can open it, after checking the sign-in. The AI services’ keys stay on the server and never reach a phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy: rozhzero969@gmail.com

من يستخدم هذا التطبيق

ريكوتا أوردرز هو تطبيق الطلبات الخاص بمطبخ ريكوتا في أربيل. يمكن فقط لحسابي موظفي المطبخ تسجيل الدخول؛ لا يوجد تسجيل عام. مديرة المطبخ، روجا، هي المسؤولة عن البيانات فيه.

لا توجد إعلانات ولا تتبع عبر التطبيقات أو المواقع الأخرى، ولا تُباع أي بيانات ولا تُشارك لأغراض تسويقية. يحتفظ التطبيق بسجلّين عن استخدامه، موضّحين أدناه: الوقت المستغرق في التطبيق، وتقارير الأخطاء.

  • تسجيل الدخول: رمز جلسة (مشفّر)، ومعرّف عشوائي لهذا الجهاز، ونوعه (مثل iPhone)، وإصدار التطبيق ووقت آخر استخدام. تُحفظ كل محاولة لإدخال الرمز مع صيغة مشفّرة من عنوان الشبكة، لمنع تخمين الرموز.
  • عمل المطبخ: الموردون (الأسماء وأرقام الهواتف وأوقات التذكير)، والأصناف، والوحدات، والطلبات (ما طُلب ومن طلبه ومتى)، والمسودات غير المرسلة، وسجل بمن غيّر الأصناف والموردين، وسلسلة المطبخ، وملخص كل شهر منتهٍ.
